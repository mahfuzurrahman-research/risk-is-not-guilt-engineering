## Claim limits

This supports hands-on Python/scikit-learn classification, anomaly scoring, temporal validation, queue prioritization, and testing experience on fabricated data.

Do not quote synthetic AP/AUC as real fraud-detection performance. The anomaly mixture does not improve queue precision over classifier-only ranking in the recorded default run.
